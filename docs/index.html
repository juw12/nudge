See who's waiting on a reply.

Friends share reels and messages while we scroll past them. Nudge analyses your own chat exports and shows which conversations need your attention, with a suggested reply for each.

  • Runs entirely in your browserYour chats are never uploaded. No account, no tracking.
  • Works with your existing appsInstagram, WhatsApp, Messenger and Telegram exports.
  • Stays up to dateConnect your Downloads folder and new exports are picked up automatically.

How to export your chats

Instagram
  1. Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Select Messages, format JSON, and a date range such as the last month.
  3. When the email arrives, download the .zip to your connected folder or drop it here. No need to unzip.
WhatsApp
  1. Open a chat → tap the contact name (iPhone) or ⋮ (Android) → Export chat → Without media.
  2. On Android with Nudge installed, choose Nudge in the share sheet. It imports directly.
  3. Otherwise save it to your connected folder or drop the .zip / .txt here.
Messenger
  1. Facebook → Settings → Accounts Center → Download your information → Messages, format JSON.
  2. Drop the .zip here or save it to your connected folder.
Telegram
  1. Telegram Desktop → Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data → Personal chats, Machine-readable JSON.
  2. Drop result.json here.
iMessage
  1. Browsers cannot read the iPhone message database. Use the Nudge command-line tool: nudge digest imessage.
