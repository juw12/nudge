nudge

Who's waiting on you?

Friends keep sending you reels while you scroll past them. Nudge reads your own chat exports and shows who you've been leaving on read, with a message ready to send.

No more dragging

  • 🔒 Nothing leaves your device
  • ✈️ Works offline
  • 🙅 No account, no tracking

How to get your chats

Instagram
  1. Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Some of your information → Messages, format JSON, date range e.g. last month.
  3. When the email arrives, download the .zip and drop it here. No need to unzip.
WhatsApp
  1. Open a chat → tap the name (iPhone) or ⋮ (Android) → Export chat → Without media.
  2. Android with nudge installed: pick nudge in the share sheet. Done, nothing to save.
  3. Otherwise save it to Downloads (nudge finds it if the folder is connected) or drop the .zip / .txt here.
Messenger
  1. Facebook → Settings → Accounts Center → Download your information → Messages, format JSON.
  2. Drop the .zip here.
Telegram
  1. Telegram Desktop → Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data → Personal chats, format Machine-readable JSON.
  2. Drop result.json here.
iMessage

Browsers can't open the iPhone message database. Use the nudge command-line tool on your computer: nudge digest imessage.
